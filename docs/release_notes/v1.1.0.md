Trained weights, the shielded closed-loop benchmark and FPV demonstration videos, all public.

**Hugging Face**
- Weights (GPL-3.0): [ylee-sling/aero-vpa](https://huggingface.co/ylee-sling/aero-vpa). This is the six DAgger checkpoints (r1 recommended), the three behaviour-cloning baselines and the two stage (i) checkpoints, with six FPV videos.
- Benchmark dataset (CC BY 4.0): [ylee-sling/aero-vpa-benchmark](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ylee-sling/aero-vpa-benchmark). It holds every mission (a Parquet table and the raw records), the decision traces, the 36 test worlds and the reports.

**Results** (PX4 SITL + Gazebo Harmonic, 36 test worlds × 5 missions, paired; computed from the published records)
- With the onboard safety shield (no map), r1 succeeds in 52.2% of missions [45.0, 59.4], against 21.1% without it (exact McNemar p = 1.2 × 10⁻¹⁰).
- kw succeeds in 50.0%, against 15.6% (p = 4.7 × 10⁻¹²).
- All six checkpoints reach 42.8–52.2% with the shield. Their crash rates fall from 72–82% to 22–43%.
- On the missions the shield was not revised on (missions 1–4), r1 succeeds in 52.1% and kw in 54.2%.
- These results are from simulation only.
